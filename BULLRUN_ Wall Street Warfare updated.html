How trading works

1. Pick a stock or coin on the left. 2. Set an amount and press Buy. 3. Sell later for more than you paid to make a profit. Prices react to news and to your missions: win a contract and the defended stock jumps while the target drops. Crypto swings hard, so it can pay big or lose big. Trading is locked during missions.
